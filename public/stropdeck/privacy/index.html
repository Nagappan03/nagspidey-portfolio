Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Stropdeck is an app for practising AI engineering interview questions with flashcards. It is run by Nagappan S, India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who else handles it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, write to stropdeck@nagspidey.co.in.

What we collect

You can read the flashcards without an account. If you create an account, we collect:

InformationWhy we need it
Email address and passwordTo create your account and sign you in. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash by our sign-in provider; we cannot read them.
First and last nameTo show your name in the app and personalise it.
Date of birthOnly to confirm you are 18 or older.
Mobile numberTo allow one account per number, which prevents repeated misuse of free trials. We do not send you SMS and we do not verify the number by SMS.
Gender (optional)To personalise the app. You can leave it blank.
Profile photo (optional)To show your picture in the app. It is resized on your phone before upload.
Google account details (only if you use Google sign-in)Your name, email address and profile picture from Google, to create your account.
Notification token (only if you turn on notifications)A code that identifies your phone, so we can send you notifications about new topics and features. It is stored with your account and deleted when you turn notifications off or delete your account.
Sign-in session and consent recordTo keep you signed in, and to record when you accepted the Terms.

The app also saves a copy of the flashcards on your phone so they work offline. We do not use advertising, we do not use third-party analytics or tracking in the app, and we do not sell your information.

Who handles your information

We use these providers to run the app. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in system and photo storage.
  • Resend delivers the sign-in and verification emails we send you.
  • Google provides Google sign-in, if you choose it, and distributes the app through Google Play. Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers notifications to your phone if you turn them on.
  • Expo runs the push notification service that sends our notifications through Google, and builds the app.

Some of these providers process data on servers outside India. We do not share your information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, photo and sign-in record are deleted straight away. Copies in routine backups are removed as the backups expire. Emails we receive from you are kept only as long as needed to answer them.

Your choices and rights

  • You can view and edit your details, change or remove your photo, and sign out in the app's Account section.
  • You can delete your account inside the app (Account, then Delete my account), or request deletion at this page.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or withdraw your consent at any time by writing to stropdeck@nagspidey.co.in. Withdrawing consent means we can no longer keep your account.
  • Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you also have the right to have a grievance addressed. Write to us at the email above and we will reply within a reasonable time, normally within 30 days.

Children

Stropdeck is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, access to the database is restricted by per-user rules, and passwords are hashed. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

New features and changes to this policy

We plan to add features such as AI-powered practice. Before any such feature collects new information, we will update this policy and the date above, and we will tell you in the app if the change is significant. Using the app after an update means you accept the updated policy.
