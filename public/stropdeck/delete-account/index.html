Delete your Stropdeck account

You can delete your Stropdeck account and its data at any time.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Stropdeck and tap your picture (or "Sign in") at the top right.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm twice. Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2: by email

If you can no longer open the app, email stropdeck@nagspidey.co.in from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my Stropdeck account". We will confirm and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details (email, password hash, Google link)
  • Your profile: name, date of birth, gender, mobile number and consent record
  • Your profile photo
  • Usage records tied to your account

Your mobile number becomes available for use on another account. Copies in routine backups are removed as the backups expire. We keep nothing about you after that, except an email you sent us for as long as we need to answer it.

Deleting your account cannot be undone. The free flashcards keep working without an account.
